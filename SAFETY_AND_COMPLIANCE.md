* High Voltage Warning:

The Model 101X is a vacuum tube amplifier operating at lethal high voltages exceeding 250V DC.

These voltages are capable of causing:
- Severe electric shock
- Permanent injury
- Cardiac arrest
- Death

This equipment must be treated with the same level of caution as industrial or mains electrical infrastructure.
Do not touch, probe, modify, or service this device while it is powered or connected to mains voltage.
Capacitors within the power supply may retain dangerous charge even after the device is switched off. Always assume high-voltage presence until properly discharged using appropriate procedures and equipment.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Safety Requirements for Service or Construction:

This design is intended for qualified individuals only. Do not attempt to build, modify, or service this amplifier unless you have:
- Formal or practical experience with high-voltage electronics
- Understanding of vacuum tube circuit operation
- Proper insulated tools rated for mains and high-voltage work
- A high-voltage rated multimeter
- A current-limited power source or VARIAC for testing
- Knowledge of safe discharge procedures for filter capacitors
- A controlled and isolated work environment

If you do not meet these requirements, do not attempt construction or servicing under any circumstances.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Operational Safety:

- Never operate the amplifier without a properly connected load (speaker or dummy load)
- Do not bypass grounding or safety earth connections in production units
- Do not operate the amplifier with exposed internals
- Ensure proper ventilation during operation; tubes operate at high temperatures
- Allow sufficient cooldown time before handling internal components
